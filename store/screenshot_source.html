MODEL GRABBER

Save your Tripo3D models in one click.

Model Grabber watches for .glb, .gltf, .fbx, .obj, .usdz and .stl files as they load in Tripo3D Studio, and lists them for instant download — no digging through DevTools.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is ever collected or sent anywhere else.
